2026 Playmakers CupStudent-Athlete Assistance

Need-based support to help a player stay in the game. One participant in the 2026 Playmakers Cup will receive up to $500 toward documented club soccer fees or soccer equipment.

Assistance
Up to $500
Recipients
One player
Eligibility
Grades 6–12
Apply by
November 20, 2026

Before you apply

  1. You must be in grades 6–12 and a registered player participating with a team in the 2026 Playmakers Cup. We will verify participation against tournament records.
  2. Explain your financial need and how the assistance would help. You do not need to submit tax returns, bank statements, or receipts with this application.
  3. A parent or legal guardian must review and acknowledge applications for players under 18.

How payment works

The selected recipient must provide receipts for paid club soccer fees or soccer equipment. We reimburse documented eligible expenses up to $500. Receipts totaling $500 or more support the full $500 award; otherwise, payment is limited to the documented amount. A parent or guardian handles reimbursement for a minor.

Applications close November 20, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific. Selection is based on demonstrated financial need and the application. Publicity permission is optional and does not affect eligibility.
