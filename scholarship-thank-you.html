Playmaker Sports Foundation Scholarship

Thank you for applying.

Applications for the 2026 Playmakers Cup scholarship will be reviewed based on demonstrated financial need. Here are the scholarship details and what happens next.

Scholarship details

Application deadline
November 20, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific.
One recipient · Up to $500
Need-based reimbursement for paid club soccer fees or soccer equipment and gear, up to the amount supported by eligible receipts.
Eligible players
Registered participants in the 2026 Playmakers Cup who are in grades 6–12.

What happens next

The Foundation will review applications and verify tournament participation. The selected recipient will be notified by Saturday, November 28th with more details.

Only the selected recipient will be asked for receipts before reimbursement. A parent or legal guardian handles payment arrangements for recipients under 18.

If selected, you or your guardian will be contacted about the next steps. You do not need to send receipts now. Submission does not guarantee an award.
