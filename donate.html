Why Your Gift Matters

Across Greater Southern California, thousands of young athletes are unable to participate in organized sports due to rising costs. Registration fees, equipment, travel, and training expenses have made youth sports increasingly inaccessible to families facing financial hardship.

Playmaker Sports Foundation was created to change that. Every dollar you contribute helps remove the barriers standing between a young athlete and the game they love — and the opportunities that come with it.

How Funds Are Used

100% of program-directed donations fund direct services for youth athletes — scholarships, free clinics, equipment distribution, leadership programs, and coaching education. We are committed to responsible stewardship of every contribution we receive.

Tax-Deductible Giving
Playmaker Sports Foundation is a federally recognized tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN on file). All contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. You will receive a receipt for your records.
